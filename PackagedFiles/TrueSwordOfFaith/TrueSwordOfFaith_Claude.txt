  How Armoury of the Expanse does it

  Armoury of the Expanse is the same kind of mod as yours (an Owlcat
  "Modifications" mod with Blueprints/ and OwlcatModificationSettings.json). It
  also ships a small C# DLL in Assemblies/. Inside that DLL I found:
  - OwlcatModificationEnterPointAttribute: the entry point the game calls when
    it loads the mod.
  - UnityModManager, ModEntry, GUILayout, Toggle, MyOnGuiMethod: these draw its
    options in the Ctrl+F10 window.
  - ApplyPatchesFromSettings: it chooses which blueprint patches to apply based
    on the saved settings (ArmouryOTE_settings.json holds toggles like
    loreInaccurateLascannon).

  Your mod would follow the same pattern:
  1. Add a DLL to TrueSwordOfFaith/Assemblies/ with an
     [OwlcatModificationEnterPoint] method. It hooks an OnGUI function into the
     mod manager, which gives you a Ctrl+F10 panel with a checkbox such as
     "Apply Sword of Faith ability changes (no momentum gain, no minimum
     momentum requirement)".
  2. Save the checkbox state to a small settings file, like
     TrueSwordOfFaith_settings.json next to the other mods' settings files.
  3. Skip the patch when the box is off. At startup the DLL reads the setting
     and removes the SancticSwordOfFaithAbility entry from the mod's patch list
     before blueprints load. The other five patches stay unconditional.
